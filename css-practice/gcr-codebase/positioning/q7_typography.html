On the Quiet Discipline of Reading

A short piece on typography and legibility

Reading well-set text feels effortless, but that ease is built from careful choices: a generous line-height that gives the eye room to travel from one line to the next, comfortable letter-spacing so words neither crowd nor drift apart, and alignment that keeps every line settling naturally against the left edge. Notice how the first letter of this paragraph is enlarged, and the first line is styled differently, using the ::first-letter and ::first-line pseudo-elements.

Good typography rarely calls attention to itself. It simply gets out of the way, letting the content carry the page.
